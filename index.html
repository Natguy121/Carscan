Your floor plans

Walk around a room, touch your phone to each wall, and get a measured plan.

How scanning works
  1. Stand at any wall. Hold your phone flat, screen up, and press its long edge against the wall with the top pointing the way you're about to walk.
  2. Hold still for a moment and tap Capture wall. Type its length if you measured it.
  3. Walk to the next wall the same way round and repeat until you're back where you started.
  4. Tap Finish room. Corners are squared up and the plan closes itself. Two walls can be left unmeasured — they're worked out from the rest.

Your phone's motion sensors measure the angle of every wall. They can't measure distance, so wall lengths come from a tape measure, a laser measure, or pacing it out. Nothing is uploaded — plans are saved in this browser.
